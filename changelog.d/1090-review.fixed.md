Session-tree teardown now ends child processes before the parent when `detach_from_process` requests termination, prevents new child adoption throughout detach, and clears output subscriptions whenever a session is removed, including during restart. Tree-aware waits reselect when a chosen child resumes or is closed while its answer is being read, preserving the original timeout. Closing a previously released child no longer claims its process was terminated (#1081, #1090).
