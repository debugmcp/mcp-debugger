**JavaScript launch options report how they were handled** — `start_debugging` now warns about
ignored, malformed and unrecognized options from `dapLaunchArgs` and `adapterLaunchConfig`,
with spelling suggestions. `envFile` values override inherited environment variables, explicit
`env` values take precedence, and `null` removes a variable. An entry stop requested through
`adapterLaunchConfig` stays paused and is preserved on restart (#709, #791, #792).
