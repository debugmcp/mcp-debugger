**Attach to a Flutter app running on an Android device or emulator** — `attach_to_process` with `adapterConfig: { runner: "flutter", deviceId: "emulator-5554" }` and no URI now goes out as `flutter attach -d <id>`, which discovers the app's VM service through the device's log and forwards it itself: the attach answers in about 5 s and breakpoints hit right after. The URI another `flutter run --machine` reports in `app.debugPort` is that tool's own DDS proxy and never connects from a second tool, which is why the recipe the guide gave for desktop apps hung on a device; `deviceId` now counts as naming an attach target (so `create_debug_session` can attach in one call with it), the attach answer's `warning` says the app is being discovered, and a desktop `deviceId` without a URI is refused up front with the URI hint instead of waiting out the verification deadline. The probe app gains a periodic timer line (`BP-TICK`) so an attached app reaches a breakpoint on its own, and the Flutter e2e suites gain attach cases on the desktop (by URI) and the emulator (by device) (#882)
