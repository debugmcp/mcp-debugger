**Provenance-tracked step lifecycle, refusal recovery, and truthful step disclosures** — refused DAP step requests (`DapResponseError` before resume, such as `netcoredbg` `0x80004005` on async iterators) now keep the session `paused` instead of transitioning it to a terminal `error` state; steps whose program exits before pausing report `"The step did not pause. …"` with `exitCode` (including Dart per-expression repeat loops); pending steps avoid promising completion when an engine drops a step and remain visible to `wait_for_stop`; every adapter now captures the step's starting stack to disclose anomalous landings (`step_out` not returning or dropping multiple frames, `step_into` entering no function with engine step-filter hints, and fiber/stack switches) and prevent `changedVariables` from diffing distinct calls across `step_out`, different call sites, or broken caller unwinds; `pausedFrameNote` and non-boilerplate adapter step descriptions (such as COBOL PERFORM summaries) surface on top-level `note`, and known engine step losses and step-filter defaults are documented (#1177, #1178, #1179, #1180, #1181, #1182, #1183, #1184, #1185, #1190)
Step refusal recovery is scoped to the outstanding request, so a late refusal cannot overwrite a newer stop, resume, or session. Terminal races preserve an already observed pause; provenance comparisons and variable snapshots respect thread identity, and repeated unresolved caller unwinds continue to emit full locals.
