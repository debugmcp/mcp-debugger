**JavaScript attach by `processId` enforces its 5,000 ms inspector wait as a strict bound** — post-signal polling previously checked `deadlineMs` only between `/json/list` probes, allowing a probe still in flight at the deadline to run to its own timeout and overshoot the reported wait by a second or more. Each probe's timeout is now capped by the remaining time, no probe starts once nothing remains, and any probe or ownership query still in flight at the deadline is cut off and fails on time (#1339)
