**A failed attach or launch quotes the debug adapter's last stderr line** — when the adapter exited or dropped its DAP connection while an attach was being verified, the answer said only `debug adapter exited during attach verification (code=null)`; js-debug's own `Error: Could not connect to debug target at http://localhost:5678: Could not find any debuggable target` went to the proxy log and nowhere else. The proxy worker now sends the adapter's last sanitized stderr lines with its terminal status, and the message says how the adapter ended and quotes the line that matters: `Failed to attach: The debug adapter ended the session during attach verification: it closed its DAP connection; its last stderr line: "Error: Could not connect to debug target at …"`. A proxy that dies during a launch carries the same line (`Debug proxy exited unexpectedly during launch (code=1); adapter stderr: "…"`) (#897)
