**`pause_execution` answers in the same shape as the step tools and `wait_for_stop`** — `pending`, `message`, `stopReason`/`rawStopReason`, `location` (now with its source `context`), and the new `threadId`/`note` sit at the top level instead of only under `data`, which keeps the controller's own copy. A pause that landed on a thread other than the one the debugger reported names that thread and says why in `note` (#906, #904)
