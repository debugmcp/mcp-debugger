**Detaching from a CodeLLDB target on Windows no longer kills it after a step** — CodeLLDB's Windows detach resumes every thread with the resume state its last stop left behind, and after a `step_over`/`step_into` that is still "stepping", so the thread single-stepped with no debugger attached and the process died with `STATUS_SINGLE_STEP` (`0x80000004`) — measured every time on Windows 11 with CodeLLDB 1.11.8, on `detach_from_process` and on `close_debug_session` alike (LLDB's `ProcessWindows::DoDetach`; reported upstream as llvm/llvm-project#227099). For C/C++ and COBOL attach sessions, and any C/C++, Rust or COBOL launch detached with `detach_from_process`, the proxy now sends a DAP `continue` on the last stopped thread ahead of every detaching `disconnect` — the session's detach, the close's auto-detach and the shutdown path — and the detach result reports `data.resumedBeforeDetach: true`; if the debugger refuses the continue the detach still proceeds and `data.warning` says why the target may not survive. `detach_from_process` with `terminateProcess: true` now tells the adapter to terminate the target instead of detaching from it, and the session's own `disconnect` is no longer followed by a second one from the worker's shutdown (#763)
