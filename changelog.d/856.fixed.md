**On Windows, the end of a Rust, C/C++ or COBOL program is reported when it happens, not 2 s later** — the proxy holds a program's exit until the output it printed on the way out has been forwarded, and took "the adapter's stdio pipes closed" as the sign of that. CodeLLDB outlives its debuggee and keeps those pipes open until the session is torn down, so on Windows (where the debuggee writes straight into them) the wait ran to its 2 s backstop at the end of every program: `list_debug_sessions` kept saying `running`, `wait_for_stop` kept blocking, and a `start_debugging` of a short program answered `running` with `pending: true` for a program that had already ended. For an adapter that outlives its debuggee the proxy now takes the pipes going quiet as the sign — 100 ms without output, counted from the adapter's `exited` and re-checked after a full turn of the event loop; the longest pause measured inside 9 MB of exit-time output was 14 ms idle and 39 ms with every core busy — so the end is reported about 100 ms after the fact and output that is still arriving keeps the wait open. The 2 s backstop is unchanged, as is rdbg, whose pipes do close with the program (#856)
