**`outputTail` on a non-zero exit keeps the program's failure cause and drops adapter housekeeping** — `outputTail` previously took the last 8 lines of the session's raw output buffer across every DAP category, splitting each chunk independently, so adapter `console` lines (js-debug's launch command, CodeLLDB's `Process exited with code N.`, Delve's `Type 'dlv help'…` / `Detaching`, rdbg's REPL banner, Dart's `Exited (N).`, logpoints, and `[mcp-debugger]` notices) and runtime debugger banners (`Debugger attached.` / `Waiting for the debugger to disconnect...` on Node's `stderr`, `Listening for transport dt_socket at address: N` on a launched JVM's `stdout`) took slots in the tail, partial chunks split lines in the middle, and long `stderr` traces or dumps (Node's CJS loader frames, GnuCOBOL's `libcob` storage dump) pushed the leading error line above the 8-line cut. Consecutive chunks of the same category are now joined before line-splitting (preserving cross-category boundaries for `outputBeforeFailure`), and `outputTail` now quotes only the program's `stdout`/`stderr` lines (excluding `console` and policy-identified adapter banners), preferring `stderr` over `stdout` and keeping the first 4 and last 3 lines separated by `"..."` when the last contiguous `stderr` block exceeds 8 lines (#1277)
