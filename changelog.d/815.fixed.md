**`start_debugging` no longer sits out a 30 s ceiling on a launch that never stops** — for every adapter but js-debug, a launch with nothing armed to stop it (no breakpoints, no entry stop, a server or a long loop) waited the full 30 s readiness ceiling and then answered `state: "running"` with no word about the wait: those policies count only a pause as ready, and the proxy reports its configured state before the wait can listen. The wait now depends on what the launch has armed: with breakpoints (a logpoint counts when the adapter does not run it on), function breakpoints, `stopOnEntry` or `breakOnExceptions: "all"`, the first stop still gets the full ceiling, so a launch that reaches a breakpoint keeps answering `paused` with its reason; with nothing armed (the `"uncaught"` default does not count — a crash is not a stop that comes soon) the wait is a 5 s grace window. Either way, when the ceiling passes with the program still running the response says so — `pending: true` at the top level like the step tools, and a message naming what was not reached ("still running after 30s without reaching 2 breakpoint(s)") or why no stop is coming soon and what to do instead — and `restart_debugging` answers the same way. The "adapter may have failed to start" warning is logged only when the session is still initializing at the ceiling (#815)
