A launch that fails after its debug adapter started is reported once. The proxy worker used to send a second copy after its teardown, with `adapter PID=none exitCode=n/a` in place of the real PID and exit code, and that degraded copy overwrote the first as the session's last proxy error. Separately, the proxy worker no longer writes its log to stdout: when it runs as the worker, its loggers skip the console transport, so the per-session `proxy-<sessionId>.log` is its log and nothing fills a pipe no one reads (#967).
