**Dart and Flutter debugging, the tenth language** — a new `@debugmcp/adapter-dart` (language id `dart`, bundled in `@debugmcp/mcp-debugger`) drives the SDK's own debug adapters, `dart debug_adapter [--test]` and `flutter debug-adapter [--test]`, behind a TCP-to-stdio bridge; nothing is vendored — the Dart or Flutter SDK on the machine is the debugger (`DART_SDK` / `FLUTTER_ROOT`, the project's fvm pin, or PATH; `mcp-debugger doctor dart` shows what was found and the devices and emulators it sees). The `runner` (`dart`, `dart-test`, `flutter`, `flutter-test`) is detected from the nearest `pubspec.yaml` and the program path; an explicit `runner` wins. Measured through the server and covered by host e2e suites: Dart programs and `dart test` files; Flutter widget and integration tests and `flutter run` on Windows, macOS and Linux desktops (`deviceId`), in Chrome or Edge (headless too), on Android emulators and devices, and on the iOS simulator; conditional breakpoints and logpoints, `Unhandled`/`All` exception filters, `noDebug`, `stopOnEntry` as a temporary breakpoint on `main`; `hot_reload` (Flutter hot reload) and a hot `restart_debugging` once the app has started. Attach by VM-service URI or service-info file, or — for an app on an Android device — by `deviceId` alone (#882); a Flutter launch that misses its device answers with the devices that exist (#877); closing a `flutter run` session stops the app (#885); a simulator or desktop `deviceId` without a URI is refused up front (#1020); the tool's daily update banner no longer breaks the launch (#1026); `step_over` repeats the SDK's per-token steps until the line changes (#879). The Docker image carries the Dart SDK (Dart in-container; Flutter is host-only). CI runs Dart and Flutter host lanes beside the Linux/Windows matrix. Guide: `docs/dart/README.md`; the measured facts: `docs/dart/spike-notes.md` (#790)
