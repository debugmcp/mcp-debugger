**A per-debug-session idle policy replaces the MCP-session reaper as what ends an abandoned debug session.** On `http`, a debug-session tree that no tool call has named for `MCP_DEBUG_SESSION_IDLE_MS` (default 30 minutes; `0` disables) is closed through the normal close cascade — a launch root closes its subtree, an attach root releases its targets — unless a client is attending the process (an open SSE stream), in which case nothing expires; a tool call naming any session in a tree refreshes the whole tree, an in-flight `wait_for_stop` holds it, and children never expire on a clock of their own. The sweep runs every `MCP_DEBUG_SESSION_IDLE_SWEEP_MS` (default 60 s). On `stdio` the pipe is the lifetime and the policy defaults to off (the same variable turns it on). The HTTP reapers (`MCP_HTTP_STALE_SESSION_MS`, `MCP_HTTP_STREAM_LOST_SESSION_MS`) still reap abandoned MCP sessions, but reaping one no longer closes any debug session; a crashed client's sessions end when the idle window runs out, or sooner by an explicit close or the debuggee's exit.
