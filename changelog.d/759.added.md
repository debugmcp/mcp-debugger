**COBOL debugging (GnuCOBOL + CodeLLDB)** — a new `@debugmcp/adapter-cobol` (language id `cobol`, bundled in `@debugmcp/mcp-debugger` and preloaded in the Docker image, which now installs `gnucobol3`). Launch a `.cob`/`.cbl` source (auto-compiled with `cobc -g -fdump=ALL -A "-O0 -gdwarf-4"` into `.debug-mcp/cobol/`, with `dialect`, `format`, `copybookDirs`, `cobcFlags`, `runtimeChecks` and `stdinFile` launch options) or a prebuilt executable (its symbol manifest is regenerated from `sources` by a translate-only `cobc -C`). A Node DAP shim between mcp-debugger and the vendored CodeLLDB engine turns the compiler's own dump-routine metadata into COBOL-shaped debugging: `WORKING-STORAGE` / `LOCAL-STORAGE` / `LINKAGE` scopes with DISPLAY, COMP, COMP-3, COMP-5, COMP-1/2, group, OCCURS (including DEPENDING ON), REDEFINES and 88-level items decoded to their COBOL values (invalid packed data is shown as `<invalid packed: 0x…>` rather than crashing), `evaluate_expression` on data-names with `OF`/`IN` qualification, subscripts and reference modification, breakpoints in copybooks, statement-granular `step_over`/`step_into`/`step_out` (the engine alone stops on every generated-C line), and a `cobol_runtime_error` exception filter that pauses on libcob runtime errors (subscript out of bounds, non-numeric data — the S0C7/SSRANGE analogues) before the abort. Milestone M2 adds attach by PID with the manifest regenerated from `sources` (the process is not touched), data-division scopes served from the nearest COBOL frame when a job is paused inside libcob, `runner: "cobcrun"` to run module-only builds under GnuCOBOL's module loader, and `-m` modules named after their PROGRAM-ID so a dynamic `CALL` resolves them on Linux. Milestone M3 adds COBOL-shaped control flow: `step_over` on an out-of-line `PERFORM` runs the performed range (loops included) and stops on the statement after it, `step_out` inside a performed paragraph returns to the statement after the PERFORM that entered it, `set_breakpoint {function: "1000-INIT"}` binds paragraph, section and PROGRAM-ID names (qualifiable as `1000-INIT OF PAYROLL`), and logpoints interpolate `{WS-NAME}` COBOL data references without pausing. Verified against GnuCOBOL 3.2 (Windows MSYS2, Ubuntu 26.04) and 3.1.2 (Ubuntu 24.04) (#759)
