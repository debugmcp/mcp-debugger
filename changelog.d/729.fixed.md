**Dry runs no longer fail with `Proxy exited during initialization. Code: 0` when the proxy's exit beats its `dry_run_complete` message** — Node can dispatch a child's `exit` before IPC messages the child wrote just before exiting, and the proxy process wrapper tore its message forwarder down on that raw `exit`, dropping the acknowledgement; the wrapper now holds the exit until the IPC channel reports EOF (`disconnect`/`close`, capped at 1 s), so `exit` is delivered only after every message the child sent. A code-0 dry-run exit with no acknowledgement at all remains a loud failure (#729)
