**JavaScript: Vite's module bindings no longer fill the locals of a stop under vitest.** Vite's SSR runner, which vitest runs every module through, binds each import of a module as `__vite_ssr_import_<n>__` (beside `__vite_ssr_exports__`, `__vite_ssr_import_meta__` and others) in the scope a function closes over. A dozen of them took the stop snapshot's slots before the function's own variables ("17 variable(s) omitted") and padded `get_local_variables`. They are left out like the other names a compiler or the debugger puts in scope; `includeSpecial: true` still lists them (#1215).
