**`IDebugAdapter.translateErrorMessage` is gone from `@debugmcp/shared`** — all eleven adapters implemented it, but nothing ever called it, so the sentences it held never reached a caller, and wiring it in would have made answers worse: its "not found" mappings duplicated the messages that `validate()`, the launch gate and `validateEnvironment()` already report (and those are more specific — Python's lists the paths it tried), and several of its patterns were broad enough to mislabel other errors (JavaScript's "not found" match would have called a missing js-debug a missing Node.js). A third-party adapter that still implements the method keeps compiling; the extra method is simply never called. The one hint worth keeping, `gem install debug`, now follows the tried paths in the Ruby factory's "rdbg not found" error (#974)
