`IDebugAdapter.translateErrorMessage` is gone from `@debugmcp/shared`. All eleven adapters implemented it, but nothing ever called it, so the sentences it held never reached a caller. Wiring it in would have made answers worse. Its "not found" mappings duplicate the messages that `validate()`, the launch gate and `validateEnvironment()` already report, and those are more specific: Python's lists the paths it tried. Several of its patterns were also broad enough to mislabel other errors; JavaScript's "not found" match, for example, would have called a missing js-debug a missing Node.js.

A third-party adapter that still implements the method keeps compiling; the extra method is simply never called.

The one hint worth keeping, `gem install debug`, now follows the tried paths in the Ruby factory's "rdbg not found" error.
