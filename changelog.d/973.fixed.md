`stopOnEntry: true` now pauses a .NET launch at entry, on the opening brace of `Main` with reason `entry`. netcoredbg reads the flag as `stopAtEntry`, and the adapter used to send only `stopOnEntry`, so the program ran to completion. The launch now carries the same decision under both names. A `stopAtEntry` the caller passes in `adapterLaunchConfig` still wins (#973)
