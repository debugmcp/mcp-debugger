**`childrenReleased` is explained in the answer that carries it.** When a child process was released to run undebugged — child sessions turned off with `DEBUG_MCP_MAX_CHILD_SESSIONS=0`, the per-tree cap reached, or an adoption that failed — the launch and `wait_for_stop` answers carried only the number, while a pending answer went on promising a pause at a breakpoint that only the released child could reach. Every launch, restart and wait answer with a `childrenReleased` count now ends its message with one sentence saying how many children were released, that they run undebugged, why (naming the setting when it decided), and that a breakpoint only they reach will not hit.
