**`stdio` speaks the current MCP protocol revision, 2026-07-28, beside 2025-11-25 and earlier.** The stdio transport is served through the SDK's own serving entry, which answers a client's `server/discover` probe and pins the connection to whichever era its opening message chose: a probing client — Claude Code's v2 runtime probes its stdio servers, and until now recorded mcp-debugger as a legacy peer — lands on 2026-07-28, with `subscriptions/listen` for resource notifications and no `initialize` handshake; a client that opens with `initialize` gets the 2025 era exactly as before, `resources/subscribe` included. The 29 tools, the resources and the prompt are the same in both. The server log names each 2026-07-28 client once, on its first handled request (`[MCP Server] Client identified`, with its self-reported name and version and its protocol revision), as `Client initialized` does for a 2025 client; and the stdio exit line says what happened — the client left (stdin ended), or the transport closed after an error it names. Claude Code may keep a stdio server it once saw answer as legacy pinned that way for up to seven days; restart it after upgrading, or wait it out. Step 2 of #1025; `http` follows.
