**JavaScript first stops are verified before they are called breakpoints** — under load js-debug can surface its own module-entry break as a `pause` with the "debugger statement" description at a program's, or an adopted child's, first statement, and the js policy relabelled that as a `breakpoint` stop on a line that has none (a cluster worker was reported stopped at a breakpoint on `const WORKERS = 4;`). A first-stop pause of that shape on a launch that did not ask to stop on entry is now held while the server reads the source line under its top frame: a line without a `debugger` statement is js-debug's entry break and is resumed unseen, a line with one is the breakpoint it always was, and an unreadable line keeps the previous answer.
