**A JavaScript attach by processId says what it leaves in the target** — the inspector the attach makes Node open stays open, unauthenticated, after the session ends, until the target restarts; that was documented but never said in the answers. Now the attach answer says in `inspector` whether this attach opened it (and on which address) or found it already open, and `detach_from_process` and `close_debug_session` say what one probe of that address sees (`still listening`, `no longer listening`, or `not checked`). Nothing closes it. A target started with `--inspect-port=<n>` and attached by processId without that port still fails; the message now says the signal was sent, that an inspector may now be open on another port, and that this server closed nothing (#1261, #1310)
