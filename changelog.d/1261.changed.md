**A JavaScript attach by processId says what it observed of the target's inspector** — this server does not close the inspector an attach by processId reaches, and leaves it listening, unauthenticated, when the session ends; that was documented but never said in the answers. Now the attach answer says in `inspector` either that the port refused the attach's first probe, the attach signalled the PID, and an inspector answered there after the signal, or that an inspector of the PID was already open and no signal was sent; `detach_from_process` and `close_debug_session` say what one probe of that address sees (`listening`, `not listening`, or `not checked`, where only a list with a Node target counts as an inspector). A target started with `--inspect-port=<n>` and attached by processId without that port still fails; every failure after the signal now says the PID was signalled and that an inspector may now be open on another port (#1261, #1310)
