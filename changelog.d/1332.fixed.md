**JavaScript attach by `processId` asks the inspector for `process.pid` before reading the target title** — `activateInspector` previously trusted a `[<digits>]` suffix on `/json/list`'s target title ahead of CDP `Runtime.evaluate("process.pid")`, so a script whose filename ended in `[<other PID>]` could be mistaken for that process or misnamed as holding the port. Activation now queries the inspector first (bounded by `DEFAULT_PID_QUERY_TIMEOUT_MS`, 1,500 ms), refuses another process's inspector by the PID it answered, and falls back to the title only when the query fails — reporting what title was seen and that the inspector did not answer `process.pid` instead of stating the title digits as a confirmed PID (#1332)
