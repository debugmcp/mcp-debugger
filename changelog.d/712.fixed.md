**A js-debug `startDebugging` forwarded from a child or release connection is adopted with the caller's intent** — the caller's `request`/`stopOnEntry` (and attach extras) were threaded into a child adoption only on the parent connection; a fork's request, which js-debug delivers on its parent target's socket, reached the child session manager with js-debug's bare `{type, name, __pendingTargetId}` and so read the absent `stopOnEntry` as "wait for an entry stop, then force one" (the 15 s wait and forced pause of #704, and a readiness signal that late). The enricher is now a `ChildSessionManager` option applied at the top of `createChildSession`, the one place both entry points meet (#712)
