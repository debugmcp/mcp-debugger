**`DEV_PROXY_BACKEND_ERA` chooses the protocol era the dev proxy speaks to its backend** — the proxy negotiates by default (`auto`), so against a current backend it always lands on 2026-07-28 and nothing driven through it exercises the 2025 era that most clients still speak. `DEV_PROXY_BACKEND_ERA=legacy` opens with `initialize` instead (2025-11-25); `dev_server_status` shows the era in force, and any other value refuses to start, naming the variable (#1025)
