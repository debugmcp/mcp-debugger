**`detach_from_process` and `close_debug_session` report what became of an attached process as observed.** A detach the debug adapter never acknowledged was answered `Detached from process (process still running)`, after 30 s, and a close answered `released: true` for every attach session whatever the adapter did. Both answers are now read from what the adapter did with the detaching `disconnect`: `released: true` when it acknowledged the detach (by answering, or by sending `terminated` in reply, which is how debugpy acknowledges), `processEnded: true` when it reported that the program exited, and otherwise neither, with a `warning` that the detach was not confirmed and that whether the process is still running is not known. An unacknowledged detach now answers after 10 s instead of 30 (every attach-capable adapter acknowledges in 0 to 15 ms). The detach message for the acknowledged case changes with it: `Detached from process: the debug adapter confirmed the detach, and the process was left running`. (#1148)
