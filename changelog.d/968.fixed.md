`mcp-debugger doctor <language>` no longer fails a language because other toolchains were being probed at the same time. Doctor now probes four languages at a time and the named ones first, on their own, so on a freshly booted machine or CI runner a gated verdict measures that toolchain only. The Rust probe is one `cargo -vV` instead of two shell-spawned `cargo --version` runs and a `rustc -Vv`, which also shortens the first `list_supported_languages` and the first Rust launch (#968)
