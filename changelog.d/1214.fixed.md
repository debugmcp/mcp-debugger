**A child session reads how its tree's program was started from its tree, not from itself.** A child session is adopted, never launched, so the facts a launch or attach records (launch or attach, where an attach's target lives, the transformed launch, the launch request) were absent on it, and every reader took that for a session that had launched nothing. Under vitest, `tsx` and other runners that load sources themselves, each stop, step and `get_stack_trace` on a worker carried "The compiled file the debugger binds this location through may be stale: …/dist/…" for a `dist/` the run never loads; a child of an attach to another host was treated as local (its breakpoints were checked against, and quoted from, the host's files); and a child's `step_into` and pending-step sentences did not know what the launch had asked for. Those readers now go through one view of the run, read off the tree's root (#1214).
